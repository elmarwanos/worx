A quick intro

Two teams, one launch

Glimpse has always employed cutting-edge techniques to raise companies to the pinnacle of digital marketing. Worx is Glimpse's in-house sister company, born to integrate those foundational marketing instincts into software development.

Working together, Worx focuses on writing clean and maintainable code while Glimpse prepares the platform and social networks to connect to the website upon launch. The result: digital experiences that are visually stunning and technologically robust.
