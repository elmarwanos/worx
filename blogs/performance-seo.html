Why performance is your best SEO strategy

May 2026·Worx by Glimpse

When we rebuilt the Glimpse website, we set ourselves one measure of success: Google's own Lighthouse score. Not because the number itself matters, but because of what it measures — the same signals Google uses to decide where you rank.

Speed is a ranking factor. Officially.

Core Web Vitals have been part of Google's ranking algorithm for years now. A site that loads in under two seconds doesn't just convert better — it literally outranks a slower competitor with identical content. Performance is the rare SEO tactic that also improves the product.

What actually moves the needle

In our builds, the biggest wins consistently come from a handful of habits:

  • Shipping less JavaScript — every framework you skip is a head start.
  • Serving images at the size they're displayed, in modern formats.
  • Loading fonts with display=swap so text never blocks rendering.
  • Letting the CDN do the work: cache aggressively, invalidate precisely.

Performance is a habit, not a launch task

The Glimpse site didn't hit its scores on launch day. It got there through countless improvements over months — the same way we maintain Modon, Genesis and every long-term client. Audit, fix, measure, repeat.

If your site hasn't had a Lighthouse audit in the last year, that's the first conversation we should have.
